    How to play

    The board has six columns and six families of symbols. Each column contains one symbol from every family, and every symbol is used exactly once. Use the clues to determine which six symbols belong together in each column.

    Making deductions

    • Left-click a possibility to place it.
    • Right-click a possibility to eliminate it.
    • A false placement or elimination ends the game.
    • Forced placements are filled in automatically.

    About Logos

    Logos was inspired by the "Einstein puzzle" game published by Flowix Games (which in turn was inspired by the DOS "Sherlock" game, though I never played that).

    This type of puzzle is known as the Zebra Puzzle and sometimes credited to Albert Einstein, though the veracity of that connection is unclear.
