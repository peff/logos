      How to play
      
					
      
					
      
						
      
						
      Objective
      
						
      
							The board has six rows, each containing a family of six
							symbols. Use the clues to place each family's symbols in
							the correct order, with every symbol appearing once in its
							row.
						
      

						
      
							Place every symbol correctly to prove your wisdom. But
							beware! One false move will bring your tower of deductions
							tumbling to the ground.
						
      

						
      Example
      
						
      
							
      
							
      
								
      123
      
								
      456
      
							
      		
							
								5
							
							
      
								
      123
      
								
      456
      
							
      		
							
      
								
      123
      
								
      456
      
							
      		
							
      
								
      123
      
								
      456
      
							
      		
							
      
								
      123
      
								
      456
      
							
      		
							
      
						
      

						
      
							Each of the six positions in a row shows either a large
							symbol that has been placed, or smaller tiles
							representing the remaining possibilities. Here,
							5
							is fixed in the second position and eliminated from the
							other five. The 3 has
							also been eliminated from the first and fourth positions.
						
      
						
      
					
      

					
      
						
      
						
      Clues
      
						
      
						
      
							Example
							Meaning
						
      
						
      
							
								A
							
							A large symbol on the board is fixed in its current column.
						
      
						
      
							
								A
								△
							
							The two symbols are in the same column.
						
      
						
      
							
								A
								↔
								△
							
							The symbols are in neighboring columns, in either order.
						
      
						
      
							
								A
								...
								△
							
							A is somewhere to
								the left of △, but not
								necessarily adjacent.
						
      
						
      
							
								A
								△
								II
							
							These symbols occupy three consecutive columns, with
								△ in the middle and
								A and
								II on
								opposite sides. The symbols may appear
								in the order shown or in reverse order.
						
      
						
      

						
      
							Pressing a clue will dismiss (or restore) it. This is
							not required for solving the puzzle, but may keep
							clear the path to wisdom. Press and hold a large
							symbol on the board to highlight its clues.
						
      
						
      
					
      

					
      
						
      
						
      Controls
      
						
        
							
      • Left-click a possibility to choose it.
        • 
							
      • Right-click (or Control-click) a possibility to discard it.
        • 
							
      • Choosing or discarding the wrong possibility ends the game.
        • 
							
      • When a row’s remaining possibilities force a choice, it falls into place automatically.
						
      
						
      Chalk Marks
      
						
      
							Hold Shift while clicking to make
							tentative chalk marks:
						
      
						
        
							
      • Left-click to mark a possibility as chosen.
        • 
							
      • Right-click to mark a possibility as discarded.
        • 
							
      • Chalk marks automatically affect other possibilities in the same row, but are not checked against clues or the solution.
        • 
						
      
						
      Touchscreens
      
						
        
							
      • The Tap controls
							option allows you to select the action performed by a
							single tap.
							
      • The Expanded tiles
							option makes it easier to select tiles on small
							screens.
						
      
						
      
					
      
					
      
						
      
						
      Hints
      
						
      Press
							∵
							for a hint: once to highlight a useful clue, again to
							explain the deduction, and a third time to explore a
							step-by-step proof. You can keep playing while the
							single explanation is visible; making a move clears
							it.
						
      Hints put the current game into Zen mode:
							the timer becomes ∞, mistakes will no longer end the
							game, and your run will not be eligible for the
							Pantheon.
						
      Mistakes
      
						
      After a mistake, a clue that directly contradicts your move
							is highlighted, if one exists. Press
							∵
							to enter a step-by-step proof explaining why the move
							was wrong.
						
      Proofs
      
						
      Proof mode shows how each step would affect the board,
							but does not permanently apply its moves. Dismiss the
							proof by pressing the Hint button again, restoring
							the board state.
						
      
					
      
					
      
					
      
					
				
      
			
      
			
      
				
      
					
      About Logos
      
					
      
						Logos was inspired by the
						"Einstein puzzle"
						game published by Flowix Games (which in turn was inspired by the DOS
						"Sherlock"
						game, though I never played that).
					
      
					
      
						This type of puzzle is known as the
						Zebra Puzzle
						and sometimes credited to Albert Einstein, though the veracity
						of that connection is unclear.
