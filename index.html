How to play

The board has six columns and six families of symbols. Each column contains one symbol from every family, and every symbol is used exactly once. Use the clues to determine which six symbols belong together in each column.

Making deductions

  • Left-click a possibility to place it.
  • Right-click a possibility to eliminate it.
  • A false placement or elimination ends the game.
  • Forced placements are filled in automatically.

Reading the clues

Example Meaning
A A already placed on the board is fixed in that column.
A △ The two symbols are in the same column.
A ↔ △ The symbols are in neighboring columns, in either order.
A … △ A is somewhere to the left of △, but not necessarily adjacent.
A △ II Three consecutive columns contain these symbols. △ is in the middle; A and II may appear on either opposing side.

Right-click a clue to set it aside.
